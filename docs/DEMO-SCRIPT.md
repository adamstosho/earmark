# Demo video script (90 seconds)

From PRD Section 14.1. Record on Arc mainnet with the three demo pockets, laptop (sender) and phone (family) side by side. Free tools: OBS Studio for the laptop, the phone's own screen recorder; upload to YouTube as unlisted.

## Before recording

- Sender laptop at 1280px, light theme, on `/#/send`. Save the name "Mama" for the family address in Settings, so the screens show names, not addresses.
- Family phone in a wallet's in-app browser on `/#/family`, with the sender saved as a name too.
- Food with a weekly limit; School fees request-only, approved payees only, locked to the end of term. Close any other tabs; hide bookmarks.

## Script

| Time | Screen | Voice-over |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00 to 0:10 | Landing page | "Nigerians abroad sent home about 21.8 billion dollars last year. Once it lands, they lose all say in how it is used." |
| 0:10 to 0:30 | Sender creates "Food" (20 USDC a week), then shows "School fees" (locked to end of term) | "On Earmark I fund pockets with a purpose and set the rules. School fees is locked, so even I cannot pull it back early." |
| 0:30 to 0:50 | Phone: family home, Pay 5 USDC from Food to a trader; the laptop's feed shows "Paid" | "My mother pays straight from Food. It is final in under a second, and I see it immediately." |
| 0:50 to 1:05 | Phone: Ask for textbooks from School fees; laptop: Requests, Approve and pay | "Bigger or unplanned costs become a request. One tap and the payee is paid." |
| 1:05 to 1:15 | Phone: "Fee credit" line on the family home; laptop: "Fee credit added" in the feed | "She never bought a gas token. On Arc, gas is USDC, so the pocket keeps her topped up." |
| 1:15 to 1:30 | Explorer page for the contract, the GitHub repo, closing card with the one-liner | "Live on Arc mainnet, open source. Earmark: money sent home, with a purpose." |
