# AppBar

The sticky top bar on compact and medium screens, with a back button, the title and up to two actions.

**Provide:** `title`, optional `subtitle`, `back` with its label, `actions` (IconButtons).

- It pads for the device's top safe area. From 1024px it is hidden, and pages use `.ek-page__head` for their title.
- Titles are short and name the place: the pocket name, "Requests", "Settings".
